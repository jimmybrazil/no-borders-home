Purpose

Rotaract was founded in 1968 by Rotary International. Through Rotaract clubs, those ages 18 and older come together to bring about positive peace and change within our communities through service.

Our Motto

Fellowship Through Service and Service Above Self

Our Background

Rotaract is a global movement and family of people developing innovative solutions to today's most pressing challenges!

We bring together young professionals from across the world to exchange ideas, develop leadership and professional skills, and have fun through service.

As an online global service club you can live anywhere and still be an active member of No Borders. We have two online meetings each month that focus on leadership, professional development skills, life hacks, updates on global humanitarian efforts, and advancements through Rotary. We also work together on projects in our local communities and abroad.

Past Meetings

Stephanie Woollard

In this talk, we learned about Seven Women from their CEO and Founder, Stephanie Woollard. Young Australian social entrepreneur Stephanie Woollard began Seven Women at only 22, after meeting seven disabled women operating out of a tin shed in Kathmandu struggling to make a living and enduring harsh discrimination. Since 2006, Seven Women has been working to empower marginalised Nepalese women through equipping them with the skills and know how to thrive.

Chris Jones

Chris Jones spoke with the No Borders Rotaract about his work with Rise Against Hunger. Rise Against Hunger has partnered with Rotarians around the world to package over 30 million meals around the world. Check it out to learn more about Rise Against Hunger and their partnership with Rotary. Speaker is followed by Q&A.
